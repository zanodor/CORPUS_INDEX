Abstract
The Chess Game is praised worldwide though unjustifiably as an
intellectual sport game par excellence. But at a more closer examination
chess is revealed to be a game structurally and conceptually heavily
mistaken, defective and full of failures on the side of its inventor. The
present paper examines in great details all the mistakes, defects, and
failures in its structure and conception since its invention.
