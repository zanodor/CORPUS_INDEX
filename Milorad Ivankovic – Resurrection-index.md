The Christian dogma of the Resurrection of ONE’S PHYSICAL BODY as thoughtlessly harbored and proliferated by the Christian churches is the most nonsensical idea that has ever entered human mind. And the idea itself contradicts the teachings of Jesus and his own words when he argued with the Jews:
23 The same day came to him the Sadducees, which say that there is no resurrection, and asked him, 24 saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother. 25 Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first, when he had married a wife, deceased, and, having no issue, left his wife unto his brother: 26 likewise the second also, and the third, unto the seventh. 27 And last of all the woman died also. 28 Therefore in the resurrection whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her. 29 Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God. 30 For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven. (Matthew 22.23-30).
As is well known, the angels have NO physical bodies, but are spiritual entities, and supposedly have WINGS like birds, chickens and hens, hence naturally full of parasites called Mallophaga known as chewing lice, biting lice, or bird lice.
This is the undeniable and indisputable PROOF that Jesus himself understood and taught the concept of Resurrection as a Spiritual reality only and NOT as a PHYSICAL REALITY at all.
The notion that Jesus after the Crucifixion ascended into Heaven with his PHYSICAL Body is the most majestic nonsense in history.
Therefore, it is ludicrous to claim as the unenlightened Christians do, that Jesus was “taken up into heaven” (Luke 24.51). Or as Stephen “being full of the Holy Ghost, looked up stedfastly into heaven, and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing on the right hand of God,” claimed, “I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God” (Acts 7.56). Or as the Credo of Nicene claims, et ascendit in cælum, sedet ad dexteram Patris “And (Jesus) ascended into heaven, and sits on the right hand of the Father”.
Even apostle Paul DENIED such a possibility: “I declare to you, brothers and sisters, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, nor does the perishable inherit the imperishable.” (1 Corinthians 15.50).
