Dispensational Logic and The Creation
logical issues of the dispensations lead to a new theory
about the first biblical report of creation
Csaba Ferenc Hargita hargita.csaba@gmail.com
Abstract
The goal of the essay is to review the subject thesis using simplistic approach as much as possible, identifying and analysing two logical issues of the theory, accepting the thesis of seven dispensations by Cyrus Ingerson Scofield about the history of mankind in it's original form as is. Commenting or considering criticisms of the original theory, like economism, anti-philosophism or apologetical presuppositionalism raised by Gerstner and others are out of scope of this work. The resolution of one of the issues will lead to a new set of the original amount of seven ages. Definition of the new set of ages and identification of possible parallels with the first biblical report of creation based on symbolical approach will be done in parallel. The result would lead to a solution of the second issue through and a new theory about the first biblical account of creation in the book of Genesis. Author's own translation of biblical text is used in the study wherever it is not highlighted otherwise.
Keywords
dispensations, judgments, shedding blood, dispersion, two, creation, seven, days
1. Introduction
Biblical history observes the history of mankind from the perspective of Word of God, presuming ultimate factual reliability of the sacred scripture of Christianity based on the supposed philosophical and scientific arguments of the existence of God and His most righteous nature. According to the biblical theology God is logical, ordered and these characteristics of Him are represented in the whole created physical universe as well as in the chain of events of the human history. This concept led to the idea to set up the history to progressive, sequencing series of ages based on specific conditions. This sequence does not necessarily fit to the set of ages defined by scientific history. Also, the practical and theological complexity of the subject combined with human intelligence and imagination allows to raise countless theories of many unclear themes of the biblical accounts. There are various theories around the place of garden of Eden, the identity of giants, the place of exodus, where the Holy Ark was hidden, just as some example. One of the most inexplicable questions still open: Why to have two accounts of creation right after each other in the Bible?
The way these two different subjects could be related to each other will be detailed in this essay.

2. Brief summary of the seven dispensations
The study of Seven Dispensations by Cyrus Ingerson Scofield1 is a generally accepted basic thesis of christian theology splitting the human history to seven ages from beginning, that is the creation until denouement, the atonement of the whole mankind with God. The dispensations defined by Scofield are • first - Age of Innocence, begins with the creation • second - Age of Conscience, begins after the first pair of human casted out from Eden • third - Age of Authority, begins after the Earth purified by flood • fourth - Age of Law, begins after the nations dispersed on Earth • fifth - Age of Promise, begins after redemption of Israelis from Egypt • sixth - Age of Grace, begins after resurrection of Jesus Christ • seventh - Age of Millennium, begins with personal return of Jesus Christ ending with the judgment of Great White Throne
According to Scofield each age has the following characteristics:2 • Change in the features of God's governmental relationship with man compared to previous dispensations, • Resultant change in man's responsibility, • Corresponding new and progressing revelation to effect the change, • Application of test the man whether or not man will perfectly obey God's rule by fulfilling the responsibility characterising that dispensation, • Demonstration of man's failure to fulfill his responsibility and obey the particular rule of God characterizing that dispensation • Involvement of Divine judgment because man's failure
3. Logical issues of the theory
Besides the challenges of Scofield's theory raised by Vern Poythress, Sam Storms, John Gerstner, or Keith Mathison among others, there is also an issue with general characteristics, because • The judgments are always bound to blood shedding events, in accordance to Hebrews 9:22. “without shedding of blood is no remission.” The end of the third dispensation does not fulfill this criteria as dispersion of nations should not necessarily requires blood. • The seventh dispensation is ending with the Final Judgment followed by new Heavens and Earth. According to the simple logic this means that the seventh dispensation is not the last one, for new Heavens and Earth must be considered as next age, the eighth in row as part of human history. Consequently • If dispersion following the confusion of tongues cannot be considered as the end of the third dispensation, than the next significant blood shedding event is the ten plagues over Egypt culminating in the destruction of the Egyptian armies pursuing the Israelis. • Reorganizing the dispensations in resolution of the mentioned logical issues, there is a well defined beginning, but no end for the seventh dispensation due to the lack of closing judgment in or and a cataclysm.
There should be an acceptable solution available in the Bible to these issues.

4. The two reports of creation
The two accounts of creation made many to set their thoughts on resolution. Some claim the need of two creations based on hardly explainable, foggy reasons, leading some also to the idea to place the first creation to a different planet than Earth. This is also providing ground to speculations of extraterrestrials, their wisdom, origin excluding the person of God from the whole procedure ultimately. In spite that His figure and characteristics were the fundamentals of the seeds of any liberal thinking some centuries ago. Others explain the need of two reports based on their two different focus. While cynics are not afraid to ask if is God senile to create the same thing twice? All current theories lack a core element giving the feeling of incompleteness.
The first report of the creation is described in chapter 1 until the third verse of chapter 2 in the book of Genesis. The procedure of creation is split to seven days as follows: • Day 1: Earth, waters, light, darkness • Day 2: Split of waters, skies or firmament • Day 3: Dry land, cereals • Day 4: Luminous bodies of the firmament • Day 5: Aquatics and fowls • Day 6: Land-animals and human • Day 7: Finish. The day of rest
The second report is written in Genesis chapter 2 from verse 4. In this report God has created everything in three or depending from the point of view 4 phases from the original waste land in the favor of human. The summary of the phases: • Phase 1: Human • Phase 2: Plants • Phase 3: Animals • (Phase 4: Split human to man and woman)
The two reports are very different except the final result. Nevertheless these procedures can be accommodated that can also solve the previously raised issues of the dispensations.
5. Revision of the seven dispensations
The seven dispensations will be revised in this chapter comparing them against the first biblical account of creation with the goal to find parallels if possible based on symbolisms used in the text.
5.1 First dispensation – the age of innocence
• Beginning: Creation • Condition: Not to eat from the Tree of Recognition of Good and Evil • End: Casting out from Eden
Let's see if and how can this definition fit to the events of creation of the first day.
„In the beginning Almighty created the Heavens and Earth, that was chaotic and vacant”. (Gen 1:1-2.)

The second report of creation serves as basis of the first age of Scofield's theory, where God is creating the Earth in it's completeness. Human, plants, animals dividing human to man and woman finally. The reader is also informed about rivers flowing from the garden of Eden, heading to four directions. These heads are Pison, Gihon, Hiddekel and Euphrates. Their names mean „increase”3, „bursting forth”4, „rapid”5 and „fruitfulness”6.
The divine plan was human to cultivate and watch over the garden ensuring the growth and fruitfulness of garden causing delight to both the Creator and human.
The reason how Earth could be chaotic and void if everything done by God is perfect can be answered by the next part of the biblical record:
“Darkness was there on the bottom of the depth”(Gen 1:2.)
This text can be explained in at least two ways. The first is the generally consented one, namely, that there must be something happen since the first sentence meaning some kind of source of danger for the created world. This source of danger is obviously the Evil who „Brings Light”, Lucifer, who is death and destruction. The other possible answer is more simple. However human was already alive, there were no rules to follow. Human soul was „dark” in this sense.
Also there is also a third explanation possible.
There is a reference to the first pair of humans hidden in the description of the chaotic and waste Earth. The Hebrew phrase used to define this state is tohu7 va bohu8, tohu and bohu, having it's roots in the ancient Sumerian fertility god Dumuzi and goddess Bau. Dumuzi was generally represented by the symbol of Tau after Tammuz, the Accadian version of his name. This way the phrase tohu va bohu means Tau and Bau.9
The identification of these gods with chaos and vacancy can be explained by the fact that the original home of Abram was Northern Mesopotamia, the city of Urfa.10 He had to leave it due to the Divine order, considering waste everything he left behind. Thus the forms of Dumuzi, the fertilizing god and Bau the mother goddess became hated demons of the pagan world, the literal symbols of chaos and emptiness in Hebrew traditions also used by authors of the first account of creation. Nevertheless, identification of tohu va bohu with Dumuzi and Bau in the shapes of Adam and Eva, or Ish and Isha can be also explained from the original Hebrew text telling “and the Earth *became* tohuvabohu” recalling the divine act of shaping human from the soil.

“but the Spirit of Almighty hovered the waters” (Gen 1:2.)
It is commonly accepted, that water is the symbol of soul or spirit. Having God's Spirit hovering over the waters represents the tight bound, the soft, cherish and trembling connection between human and God as the Hebrew word “rachaph” for hovering refers to be affected with the feeling of tender love and or tremor.11 Almighty is still working on human by care to become the worthy presenter of Him in the physical world, being in contact with the Creator, the inhabitants of heavens and the creatures on Earth.
“And Almighty said: „Let there be light!” and light arose. And Almighty saw that light is good and divided it from the darkness calling light as Day and the darkness as Night”. (Gen 1:3-5.)
Deriving form the second definition of darkness above, giving light to the human soul can be identified with two events. First scenario is when God performed the initial work in the garden to teach human what and how to do to care and guard the garden. The other scenario is when God gave the first commands to human. Namely, explaining what is allowed to be eaten and what not, also explaining the consequences. However, the the most feasible solution is the complex of the two scenarios.
„The Eternal Almighty instructed and warned human: „From every trees of the garden shall you eat to eat, but you shall not eat from the Tree of Recognition of Good and Evil, for in the day you eat from it you shall die to die.” (Gen 2:16-17.)
Thus, Light is obedience to any practical and theoretical instructions of God and living with Him eternally, while darkness is disobedience, death of the body and the soul, being divided from God ultimately. God equals to Light, life, joy and abundance this way, and Darkness is Lucifer, the Evil, death, sorrow, pain and death.
“Evening came than morning on the first day.” (Gen 1:5.)
The sentence suggests, that human was not able to comply with the requirements, the divine Instructions. Falling came due disobedience, eating the fruit of the forbidden tree, resulted to shed blood of innocent animals, casting humans out from Garden. Eden is being watched over Cherubs on the East avoiding future forceful invasion of sinful, unclean human.

5.3 Third dispensation – the age of authority
• Beginning: Earth, purified by Flood • Condition: Govern cleansed Earth in line with God’s will • Judgment: Dispersion by confusion of tongues
In the age defined by Scofield as the third one, the task of the mankind was to govern the Earth in line with God's will.
In spite, from the beginning of the age what we can see is disobedience. Noah blessed two of his sons and cursed the third one - who was his grandson actually - for his own weakness of lack of self-control, getting drunken and naked. Also the whole mankind was disobedient, standing together against God's will to spread around the world. They were faithless, ignoring God's promise, but agreed to erect a tower to avoid damage of people by any future flood, that was living hard in their memories based on the tales of their fathers. And mankind was proud as the tower would also serve as memento of their builders giving them fame. This all led to the divine confusion of tongues and dispersal of mankind on the Earth.
This event is defined by Scofield as the end of the Age of Authority and the beginning of the fourth Dispensation, the Age of Promise.
• Beginning: Dispersion • Condition: Faithfulness and obedience of chosen people to God • Judgment: Egyptian bondage
As highlighted before this set up of ages 3 and 4 have to be revised. Scofield's definition is argued here while dispersion is not an event causing shed of blood, that is a basic criteria of remission. Here we will examine, if the events of the two ages could be considered as a single one based on the report of the third day of creation.

Their disbelief, pride and the pharaoh's fear led to their slavery, which than resulted the events of ten plagues, exodus and the destruction of Egyptian armies finally.
Based on the scenario above, ages 3 and 4 of Scofield's seven dispensations proposed to be merged into a single age, that still could be called “Age of Authority”.

5.5 Fifth dispensation – the age of grace
This is the sixth Age according to C.I. Scofield.
• Beginning: Resurrection of Jesus Christ • Condition: Obedience and acceptance of free grace • Judgment: Purifying Judgment
Grace is fulfilled in this age, beginning with the rise again of Jesus Christ. Humans have no extraordinary requirements to comply with the expectation of God. They only have to accept the message of divine grace and follow the law of love in the footsteps of our Saviour by the decision of their free will.
“So God created the great creatures of the sea and every living thing with which the water teems and that moves about in it, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good. God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and increase in number and fill the water in the seas, and let the birds increase on the earth..” (Gen 1:20-22. NIV)
The creatures of the waters and sky represent the multitude of people. They feel comfortable with human soul, represented by waters, but also able to see the deeper, philosophical and spiritual meaning behind the things represented by the sky.

The applicability of the animals to human can be explained by the concept of soul used by Hebrew authors of the Old Testament. According to their understanding14 there is no soul in the plants, but in animals. This soul is called “chayyah” in Hebrew meaning “living with senses”, that is “thinking”. It serves the body to live. Human is still different, consisting of “nephes chayyah”, the “living soul”, that is the final result of creation of human. This type of soul is served by the body to live. This “nephes chayyah” which is also used to describe the animals in verses 20 and 21. This way the animals created on the fifth day have to be considered as types of groups of people.
At the end of this age the Lord returns for His people. All souls having or having had faith in Him will be raptured. The Antichrist will also get into power for the seven years of Tribulations.
“Evening came than morning on the fifth day.” (Gen 1:23.)
Human failed again. The end of this age is characterized by catastrophes, betrayals, wars, persecution of the followers of Christ. The age is ending with the judgment of the unbelief of the world and the apostasy of the Church by the personal return of Jesus Christ winning over Evil, casting him down to the abyss.
5.6 Sixth dispensation – the millennium
This is the seventh Age according to C.I. Scofield.
• Beginning: Personal Return of Jesus Christ • Condition: Obey to King God • Judgment: Great White Throne and New Heavens and Earth afterwards
The condition of this age is very simple. Human have to obey to the Anointed King, Jesus Christ, the God bodily presented on the Earth.
“God made the wild animals according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and all the creatures that move along the ground according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.” (Genesis 1:25. NIV)
God creates various types of animals of the land, those have to be considered as humans, like the animals of the fifth day because of the recurring usage of phrase “living soul” on this day. The animals of the land refer to people having solid ground, foundation to life, that is given by the presence of God Himself through Jesus Christ in body.
“God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.” (Gen 1:27. NIV)
The creation of human as man and woman symbolises Christ and His Bride, the Church, to govern together the rest of people truly and fairly.
“Evening came than morning on the sixth day.” (Gen 1:31.)
Human failed again. The inability of natural human to obey God was ultimately proven. Human natural heart is tending to think and do evil. The nations instigated by evil rebel against Christ and His people.

5.7 Seventh dispensation – new heavens and Earth
This is a logically necessary additional period number 8 after the seventh age according to C.I. Scofield's theory. There are no basic criteria applied for this age being not defined by him.
“Thus the heavens and the earth were completed in all their vast array. By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done.” (Gen 2:1-3. NIV)
This age is only about the righteous ones, the era when God can live with His most favored creature the way it was originally planned.
In this age people with pure heart rule above the Earth, taking care to all creatures, getting back to the start to care and guard the Garden. Evil will be down there in the abyss, but will have no chance ever to return.
There will be no more divine judgment needed for God and His law of love will be the light of the mankind. Purified human will never fall again. There will be no more evenings and mornings just like as it is written in the first account of creation.
6. Conclusion
As it could be seen, resolving the logical issue regarding the general characteristics of dispensations of C.I. Scofield's theory would result a new set of ages.
Going through the ages according to the suggested set up and fitting their events and subjects to their symbolic meanings of the first account of creation led to a new theory in the issue of two different biblical reports of creation.
However this study is based on acceptance of the basic concept of the dispensations, having no intention to deal with its critics, the result seems to confirm the validity of the original thesis. The final outcome fits to the Hebrew concept, that Garden represents abstract conclusions made on practical experiences.15 In the proposed scenario mankind went through all phases of learning and drew all necessary conclusions until the seventh day. Based on the thoughts of their purified hearts and past experiences will have no wish to eat from the tree of recognition of good and evil. The answer will be written in their hearts.
Based on the scenario described in this essay the chain of events of the first report of creation serve as a kind of abstract, brief summary, table of contents of tthe Holy Bible, or a development plan of the whole mankind through the history.
